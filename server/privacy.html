TNI Rater Autofill – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

TNI Rater Autofill is a Chrome extension used by agents of Truckers National Insurance (TNI) to fill commercial auto insurance quotes on carrier agent portals (Progressive, AmWINS Admitted Placement, GEICO). It is for TNI agents only.

What the extension handles

  • The client's quote application (PDF). Read on the agent's computer and kept only for the browser session. Its details (such as name, address, date of birth, license and vehicle information) are typed into the carrier's quote page the agent has open.
  • Carrier page content. The questions and options on the carrier's quote page, read to fill them.
  • The agent's OpenAI API key (optional; without one, TNI's own key is used on TNI's server). Stored in the extension's settings on the agent's computer, readable by the extension's own pages only, and sent only to OpenAI to authorise Luna requests. It is never logged.
  • Usage information. Which fields were filled, what the agent changed afterwards (for typed text, only whether it matched the application, never the text), buttons clicked, timing, page layouts (question text and choices, and which choice was picked), what the AI assistant was asked and decided (counts and option texts, not the application's details), the premium the carrier's page quoted (the amount, its term and payments), problems the agent reports (the text as the agent typed it, with values from the loaded application, if one is loaded, replaced where they appear exactly as written there; agents should not type client details into a report), the agent's initials and the extension version.
  • A profile of the client's business. Taken from the application when it is loaded, and again when the agent corrects it, without the client's names, contact details, street address, city, ZIP, identification numbers (USDOT, MC, FEIN, licence, VIN, licence plate), dates of birth, claims box or notes. It holds: the garaging state; the business class, years in business and under its own authority, and whether the owner drives; whether a USDOT, MC and FEIN number was given or something else was written (never the numbers) and the 60-day USDOT/MC answer; the operations answers (what they haul, radius, vehicle ownership, personal use, ELD and its brand, SR-22, filings, hazmat, moving states, team driving, loads to Canada, equipment value); each vehicle's and trailer's type, year, make, model and value; the trailer section (ownership, hitch, interchange and its limit, trailer equipment value); each driver's age (not the date of birth), licence state, CDL and its year, whether the driver is marked excluded, marital status, and gender as the agent set it in the extension (the application doesn't ask it); the coverages and limits asked for; the current insurer, policy type, limit and the month the policy expires; and whether there are claims. Its text answers (what they haul, the business class, vehicle and trailer types and ownership, the hitch, makes and models, marital status, the insurer's name and policy type, the ELD brand, the filings and the limits) are sent as written, with the application's names and anything that looks like an identification number, phone number, date or email address taken out; such text can still name someone the application doesn't, such as a shipper. Apart from such text, the profile has no names, numbers or addresses, and it is not anonymous: the state, fleet and years in business together can still point to one business.

Where data goes

  • The carrier's website: the quote answers, as the agent's own entry.
  • OpenAI (unless the agent turns the AI assistant off): the questions the extension couldn't answer with the choices the carrier's page offers, TNI's rules, and the application with the client's names, dates of birth, street address, city, ZIP, contact details and identification numbers (VIN, licence, USDOT, MC, FEIN) removed or replaced by "[client]" where they appear as written in the application's fields, in the claims box and notes too. The same detail written another way in the claims box or notes (1/2/1980 for 01/02/1980, or a phone number punctuated differently) may still be sent. What is sent in clear includes: the business details (year established, years in business and of experience, whether the owner drives, the 60-day USDOT/MC answer, the application date); the garaging state; the business class and every operations answer (what they haul, radius, ELD, filings, hazmat, equipment value); coverage choices and limits; the trailer section (ownership, hitch, interchange and its limit, trailer equipment value); vehicle and trailer type, year, make, model and value; the driver's licence state, CDL details and marital status; the current insurer, policy type, limit and dates, and whether that policy has expired; and the claims box and notes as redacted, with whether there are claims and whether non-trucking liability was asked for. Everything is sent with the agent's own key or, without one, through TNI's usage server, which adds TNI's key and neither stores nor logs the request or OpenAI's answer, and with OpenAI's storage turned off for the request. Driving record orders, exclusions, signatures and payment questions are never sent to the AI.
  • TNI's usage server: the usage information and the business profile above, sent over HTTPS with the extension's upload token, to improve the extension and to analyse TNI's own quoting (for example, which states, business classes, fleets and coverages TNI quotes, and on which carriers). Client names, numbers, addresses and text the agent types into the quote are not included; the one exception is a problem report, sent as described above. Only TNI can read the stored log, and TNI may copy it into its own private database for that analysis; the server tells anyone its version, nothing else.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or transfer data to third parties other than the services named above.
  • We don't use data for advertising, credit or lending decisions, or anything unrelated to TNI's quoting.
  • The extension never fills or sends payment or bank details.

Retention and contact

The intake is cleared when the browser closes. Usage information and business profiles are kept by TNI to maintain the extension and to analyse its quoting. Questions or deletion requests: ayaz@truckersnational.com.
