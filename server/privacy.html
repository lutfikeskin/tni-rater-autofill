TNI Rater Autofill – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

TNI Rater Autofill is a Chrome extension used by agents of Truckers National Insurance (TNI) to fill commercial auto insurance quotes on carrier agent portals (Progressive, AmWINS Admitted Placement, GEICO). It is for TNI agents only.

What the extension handles

  • The client's quote application (PDF). Read on the agent's computer and kept only for the browser session. Its details (such as name, address, date of birth, license and vehicle information) are typed into the carrier's quote page the agent has open.
  • Carrier page content. The questions and options on the carrier's quote page, read to fill them.
  • The agent's OpenAI API key (optional). Stored in the extension on the agent's computer and sent only to OpenAI to authorise AI requests.
  • Usage information. Which fields were filled, what the agent changed afterwards, buttons clicked, timing, page layouts (question text and options, no answers) and problems the agent reports.

Where data goes

  • The carrier's website: the quote answers, as the agent's own entry.
  • OpenAI (only when the agent turns the AI assistant on): the questions the extension couldn't answer, their options, and the intake details needed to answer them, sent with the agent's own key. Driving record orders, exclusions, signatures, payment details and identification numbers are never sent.
  • TNI's usage server: the usage information above, to improve the extension. Client names, numbers, addresses and anything the agent types are never included.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or transfer data to third parties other than the services named above.
  • We don't use data for advertising, credit or lending decisions, or anything unrelated to filling quotes.
  • The extension never fills or sends payment or bank details.

Retention and contact

The intake is cleared when the browser closes. Usage information is kept by TNI to maintain the extension. Questions or deletion requests: contact Truckers National Insurance.
