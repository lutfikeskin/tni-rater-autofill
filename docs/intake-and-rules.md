# Intake mappings and conditional rules

Read this when changing the client record, interpreting an intake, or adding an answer/default/exception.

## Current contract

[intake.js](../src/intake.js) reads named PDF form fields. [normalize.js](../src/normalize.js) builds the record and warnings; [schema.js](../src/schema.js) defines editable fields. Claims-box notes are retained, with limited special parsing such as missing ownership and non-trucking liability. A blank ownership box takes the notes' answer only when it is unambiguous (`ownershipFromNotes`): exactly one ownership word (owned, lease/leased, finance/financed, loan count as mentions; only owned, leased and financed are answers), not negated in its sentence ("not leased; owned outright" names two), with no trailer mention anywhere in the notes ("Trailer.\nFinanced." may describe the trailer; "Trailer leased. Truck owned." names two units); any other mention leaves `operations.vehicleOwnership` empty with an `ownership.notes` check quoting the lines, in place of `ownership.missing`. A filled box always wins over the notes. A line with NTL, non-trucking (also "non tracking") or bobtail adds a review item quoting it, and `coverage.ntl` stays unset until the agent sets it (a mention such as "no bobtail" is not a request). An intake listing a semi trailer (Dry Van, Reefer, Flatbed, Tanker, Lowboy, Step Deck) with no Semi Truck or Pickup Truck to pull it gets a check warning on the vehicle types. This is not a general free-text extractor; Luna's claims-box review (see [AI assistance](ai-assistant.md)) only flags lines.

The intake form changed on 2026-10-19 ([test/fixtures/sample-intake-2026-10.pdf](../test/fixtures/sample-intake-2026-10.pdf); the blank form and a preview are in [docs/intake-form](intake-form/)). The other-insurance-products box (`cross_*`) was removed, the claims box narrowed, and an Additional Notes box (`additional_notes`) added beside it; "Do you take loads to Canada?" (`loads_to_canada`, Yes/No) was added to the FEIN / SR22 / ELD row; and the questions under the trailer rows use the agency's wording, with a single interchange limit and the two equipment values side by side (`trl_interchange_limit`; the older second one beside the hitch, `interchange_limit`, is gone). It keeps the 2026-10-02 form's team driving / slip seating question (`team_driving_or_slip_seating`), which replaced the license plate. Every version is read: `KNOWN_FIELDS` lists the fields of each. `operations.teamDriving` and `operations.canadaLoads` are null (unanswered, not No) on an intake from before the question or when the dropdown is left on Select*; no carrier question is mapped to either yet. The Additional Notes box is `notes.text`, as written, and is read like the claims box's other lines: `otherNotes` (the claims box's notes and Additional Notes) answers a blank ownership box and feeds the defensive driver and POP-unavailable checks, and `intakeNotes` (the whole claims box and Additional Notes) feeds the NTL mention and Luna's review. Only the claims box's first line answers the claims question. `crossSell` stays on the record for older intakes and is empty on the new form.

The radius box (`parseRadius`) is read like this: "48 states" and "lower 48" alone are long haul (their 48 is not miles); any additional distance is parsed normally; unlimited, long haul, nationwide, OTR and "any" without digits are long haul; a single explicit mileage wins ("anywhere within 100 miles" is 100, "OTR up to 500 miles" is 500). Long haul gives no mileage and no warning, and both carriers pick their widest band (`isLongHaul`, the parser-based predicate they share, used only when there is no mileage); a bare "48" is 48 miles. Geographic modifiers are bounded to continental/contiguous/US; "48 in-state miles" and "48 interstate miles" remain 48 miles. Location prose after an explicit mile unit ("100 miles in CA") does not turn "in" into inches; standalone inch units remain unresolved. A plain number, with or without "miles", is `operations.radiusMiles`; `operations.radius` keeps the text as written. A range ("100-500 miles", "100 to 500", "100 miles to 500 miles", "100mi-500mi") leaves the miles empty with a `radius.range` check, unsupported or unrecognized distance unit words (kilometres, feet, nautical miles, furlongs, etc.) with `radius.unit`, and anything else ("around town", "11 states", "0", a mileage with another number after it such as "100 miles / 500 miles") with `radius.not-miles`; the check card edits the miles. Long-haul words alongside a range, unsupported unit, zero, or multiple numbers preserve that review item and never select the widest carrier band.

The owner (`owner()` in [progressive.js](../src/carriers/progressive.js), shared by AmWINS) is matched to a driver row for the date of birth and name split by the same full name, or by surname (`ownerIsDriver` in [util.js](../src/util.js)) only when the owner's first name is one of that driver's given names: owner "Alice Sample" is not driver "Bob Sample". When the intake says the owner doesn't drive (`applicant.ownerDrives` false), no row is linked at all, the name parts come from the owner box and the date of birth is the agent's. Two intake quirks stay: the sole driver whose first name is repeated in the surname box (only when the owner drives) and an owner box holding only a first name, matched to the one driver with it (not when the owner doesn't drive). A generational suffix written at the end of a driver's first- or last-name box ("SAMPLE, JR", "EXAMPLO III": Jr, Sr, II, III, IV) is read into `drivers[].suffix`, and a lone letter after the first name ("JORDAN L") into `drivers[].middle`, each with an info note (`name.suffix`, `name.middle-initial`) in place of the two-words check (TNI, 2026-10-19, after an Arkansas run where Progressive refused "<first>, JR": its name boxes take letters, digits, spaces, hyphens and apostrophes only). The name boxes then get the name alone. No carrier's driver page has a suffix box, so the suffix is left out there; Progressive's middle-initial boxes stay as the page has them (its NoHit advice is first and last name only), and AmWINS' middle name box gets the initial. The owner match counts the suffix (owner "SAMPLE EXAMPLO JR" is driver SAMPLE EXAMPLO, suffix JR). A one-letter first name ("A J") and other two-word first names stay as written, with the check.

Each answer of the record knows the form field(s) it was read from ([intake-source.js](../src/intake-source.js) `sourceFields`; a driver, vehicle or trailer by its form row, `rows` from `normalizeIntake`, since blank rows are skipped), and the PDF says where each field is drawn (`readIntakePlaces`). The panel uses them to show a check item or a filled intake answer outlined on the intake PDF; a TNI standard or a carrier-only answer has no field and no link. A `derived` answer that fills something carries the intake answers its rule read (`from` on its plan step: `readsOf` in [plan.js](../src/plan.js) runs the rule's value function once more on a view of the record that notes each value read). Blank answers it looked at are left out unless they are all it read, and a list it searched (every driver, for the owner's row) keeps only the items whose values appear in the answer. `sourcePlace` takes those paths and returns one mark per field on the first one's page, plus the box around them. It only locates; it decides no answer, and a rule that throws while traced changes nothing.

A panel edit to `operations.radius` or `applicant.yearEstablished` recomputes what was derived from it (`rederive`: `radiusMiles` through the radius parser, `yearsInBusiness` from the current year) and refreshes that row; an edit to the derived field itself is the agent's value and recomputes nothing, and for the rest of that intake a later edit to its source no longer recomputes it (the panel saves the fields the agent edited with the intake, as `agentEdited`, so they survive closing and reopening the panel, and passes them to `rederive` as `keep`; a newly loaded intake starts with none). Warnings are not recomputed after an edit.

[plan.js](../src/plan.js) resolves values before they enter the page. Rules come from [carriers.js](../src/carriers.js), [carrier modules](../src/carriers/), and [generic rules](../src/rules.js) unless the carrier disables them. Progressive disables broad generic rules.

| Rule responsibility | Current mechanism |
| --- | --- |
| Find a question | `field` (data-model name) or `ask`/`label` on the rule's `page`, `selector`, `withOption`, or `label` plus `not`; `pageOnly` keeps a data-model name to the rule's pages and `notPages` keeps a rule off pages (a trailer's questions carry the vehicle's names) |
| Supply an answer | `path` or `value(record, active)`, with its `source` (below) |
| Leave a decision to the agent | `note`; unresolved value becomes a review item |
| Deliberately preserve/skip a field | `ignore`; use only for a known reason |
| Fill but require confirmation | `warn` |
| Address repeated entities | Active list index, `repeat`, selectors, and `scope: 'vehicle'`. With several vehicles, a `scope: 'vehicle'` rule becomes one step per vehicle; each takes the one row naming its vehicle (`vehicleHint`: at least two of year, make and model), and a row's questions without the vehicle in their label belong to the vehicle its labelled questions name. On a page with no vehicle-labelled rows, only the active vehicle's step answers. A vehicle with a trailer on the quote also finds its row by name, and a `scope: 'trailer'` rule (a trailer's Rates row, "2019 Wilson") is one step per trailer, the same way. A `repeat` rule whose rows show no name but a value of the unit names it as a `witness: { selector, path }` (AmWINS' vehicle rows by `[data-id="vin"]` and `vehicles[].vin`): the plan resolves the path for each unit and the step takes the row showing that value, not the row at the unit's position. AmWINS searches all rows for the vehicle value even when the intake has only one vehicle; a unit with no such row (or two) stays the agent's, named without the value. `scopeIndex` is copied onto steps but `assign()` does not read it. |
| Interpret existing carrier values | `replace`, `trustPage`, `verifyOnly`, `emptyNote`; `keepChoice`: a question the carrier never pre-selects, so a differing choice already there is kept and flagged, not replaced (`replaceChoices`) |

### Answer provenance (`source`)

Every carrier rule with a `value` declares where its answer comes from ([test/provenance.test.mjs](../test/provenance.test.mjs) enforces it); a rule with only a `path` is `intake`. `plan.js fieldSteps` acts on it:

| `source` | Meaning | Step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `intake` | A record path, no computed value | Filled from the intake |
| `derived` | Computed from intake facts, with the evidence in the rule | Filled; may carry its own `warn` or `note` (a missing fact gives a note, never a guess); its step's `from` names the intake answers read, shown on the PDF |
| `standard` | A TNI coverage or workflow standard | Filled and marked `standard: true`; the carrier page marks it blue (an intake answer is green) and the panel's fill report tags it "standard" |
| `fact` | A client fact the intake doesn't supply (loss runs, current-carrier tenure, the year licensed without a readable date of birth, other business coverages when the intake has general liability, …) | Never filled: `value: null`, `manualOnly: true` (the AI assistant leaves it) and the rule's `note` (else "<name> isn't on the intake: answer it yourself") |
| `attestation` | Something the agent must assert (the AmWINS declaration, Progressive continuous coverage when the intake doesn't show the customer currently insured, Progressive "USDOT belongs to the business" when the intake has no business or owner name to check) | Never filled, as `fact` |

A rule's `source` may be a function of the record when the intake decides it. "No equipment" (Progressive's vehicle and trailer "has no equipment", on Add Vehicle, Add Trailer and the trailer's Rates row, and AmWINS "Permanently attached equipment" No) is `standard`, TNI's like the $0 to $2,000 band, when the intake gives no equipment value (blank or unreadable, such as N/A), so it is tagged and becomes a check item in review mode; with a value on the intake it is `derived` (Progressive: up to $2,000 Yes, more No; AmWINS: $0 No, any other amount the agent's).

A rule marked `clientFact` (AmWINS marital status, entity type, prior loss, attached equipment; Progressive business structure, has a USDOT number and vehicle / trailer loan / lease) answers from the intake when it can; when it can't (no value, or a note), its step is `manualOnly` too, so the AI assistant never suggests or picks it.

Review mode: a carrier lists the business classes its rules were written for (`supportedClasses`, the same patterns the class-dependent answers use; Progressive: an expediter or a freight hauler class ("Freight Hauler", "General Freight Hauling"), as its for-hire and business-type answers read it, so "General Freight" or a Dry Van hauling general freight fills in review mode; AmWINS: the class or what they haul names expediting or general freight, the same pattern its freight answers (484122, TRANSPORTATION, FOR HIRE) use, so a "Freight Hauler" of furniture gets both their notes and review mode). `supportedClass(record, carrier)` is false for any other class and for a missing one; then every `standard` step gets the `warn` "Review mode: the business class "…" isn't validated for <carrier>; confirm this standard answer.", so it is filled but becomes a check item that holds Continue, and the panel shows a review-mode banner above the fill button. A retained carrier choice also requires confirmation in review mode, even when it differs from the standard: its value is preserved and Continue waits for the agent to acknowledge it. Intake and derived answers are unaffected.

The 2026-10-09 classification turned several earlier TNI defaults into notes: the USDOT belongs / within 60 days answers, continuous coverage, other business coverages, the NJ vehicle registrant, blank trailer ownership as owned, blank vehicle ownership as no loan/lease, "will not have a USDOT number" with no 60-day answer, and on AmWINS gender, year licensed (birth year + 17), year established (this year), current-carrier tenure, loss runs and marital status (Married). Each became the agent's when the intake doesn't answer it; some are TNI standards again since, such as the USDOT within 60 days (2026-10-19) and belongs (2026-10-20) answers.

TNI on 2026-10-10 brought four of them back as standards (live run: a license year the agent typed was refused and AmWINS went back from Vehicles to Drivers; Progressive's current BI limit waited behind a blank continuous coverage):

- AmWINS driver gender: MALE (`standard`) unless the driver's `gender` is set; then that (`derived`). `drivers[].gender` (MALE / FEMALE) is in [schema.js](../src/schema.js) under Drivers but not on the intake PDF (`NOT_ON_INTAKE`: normalizeIntake leaves it null, `sourceFields` gives no form field); the agent sets it in All client details or by accepting an AI suggestion. Any other value there is the agent's pick on the page. Progressive's commercial driver page asks no gender.
- AmWINS year first licensed: birth year + 17 (`standard`), from the driver's date of birth (MM/DD/YYYY); a missing or unreadable date of birth is the agent's (`fact`, with a note). Progressive asks no year licensed (only the CDL year, from the intake).
- Progressive continuous coverage (`83`): Yes (`standard`: TNI's assumption, not an intake fact, so it has no PDF link) when the intake shows the customer currently insured (the same `currentlyInsured` as `81`); otherwise the agent's (`attestation`).
- Progressive other business coverages: `A2` "None", both "None of the above" boxes (`AA`, `B2`) ticked and the coverage boxes (`A7`, `A8`, `A9`, `AF`, `B0`) unticked, all `standard`, unless the client has general liability: the agent marked that they have it (`coverage.hasGl`, not on the form; set from Luna's claims-box card or in All client details), or the intake asks for GL (`coverage.gl`, Requested coverage, so whether they have it now is unknown). Then all of them are the agent's (`fact`, with a note naming which). `coverage.hasGl` is not a GL request: it changes nothing else on the quote. The boxes carry `group` (one row per group in the panel's report: "Other business coverages" and "Other business coverages (Progressive bundle)").

TNI on 2026-10-19 (live run: the agent answered all three by hand) brought back three more as standards, which TNI may change later:

- AmWINS how long with the current carrier: 1 TO 2 YEARS (`standard`), asked only of clients with a DOT number.
- AmWINS can provide claim-free loss runs: No (`standard`). Valued loss runs: No (`standard`; TNI after a later AmWINS run the same day, until then the agent's).
- Progressive USDOT obtained within the last 60 days (`21E`): Yes (`standard`). Progressive asks it only after Verify USDOT finds no record for the number, with its notice on the page.

Later on 2026-10-19 (a second SC live run: each of these went to the agent and to Luna, though Progressive often had No already selected), TNI made four Progressive answers standards for an intake that doesn't answer them. An intake answer still wins:

- Hazardous material placard (`2F6`): the intake's Yes or No; blank is No (`standard`), unless the intake says they haul hazardous material, or what they haul names something that may need a placard (fuel, propane, chemicals and the like, `HAZ_HAUL`; "non-hazardous" and "no hazmat" don't count); then the agent's, `derived` with a note. A placard question found only by its label gets the same answer.
- A driver's accidents, claims or violations (`28F5`, "past 3 years" or "past 5 years"): the claims box's Yes or No (`claims.hasClaims`); neither is No (`standard`) unless the intake's notes (the claims box's other lines, the Additional Notes box) mention an accident, claim, violation, ticket, points, a revoked license or the like (`claimMention` in [progressive.js](../src/carriers/progressive.js)), or the intake says an SR-22 is needed; then the agent's, with the line quoted. A sentence that only denies them ("No claims or violations in the last 5 years", "Clean record", "N/A"), claim-free, loss runs and a loss payee are no mention. AmWINS prior loss is unchanged (above).
- State or federal filings required (`CA`): the intake's Yes or No; blank is No (`standard`) when the intake has no USDOT and no MC number, also when it says one is coming within 60 days (TNI, 2026-10-19: the USDOT question then answers "Not Yet"); with either number, with filings named in the intake's which-filings box, or with an SR-22 needed (a state filing), a blank stays the agent's, with a note. The Filings page (shown only after a Yes) is unchanged.
- Has the insured or their hired driver had the vehicle less than one (1) year (`2C06`, Add Vehicle, first seen in SC): No (`standard`), except for a vehicle of this model year or later, likely bought within the year: then the agent's (`fact`, never Luna's). How long they have had it (`2C09`) stays the agent's.

TNI on 2026-10-20 (a PA live run: "this question appears only after we insert the DOT number and verify it") brought back the 2026-10-01 answer:

- Progressive "Does this USDOT Number belong to the customer's business?" (`20F`): Yes (`standard`) when the intake has a USDOT number; Progressive asks it only after Verify USDOT finds the number. The identity check stays: when the lookup's registered business name is on the page and isn't the intake's business name (`businessNames`), neither this question nor the Business Name choice (`22F`) is answered, and both hold Continue with "The DOT lookup business name differs from the intake". Progressive shows the registered name either beside this question or right after a Yes. When it comes after, the Yes stays on the page, but it is no longer reported as filled: it becomes the agent's to verify and holds Continue, so a wrong USDOT number stops at the name either way. A registered Business Address (`277`) picked before the name showed moves the same way. With no business or owner name on the intake there is nothing to compare, so the question is the agent's (`attestation`). Progressive never pre-selects it, so an answer already there is kept and flagged (`keepChoice`), also when the page marks it invalid. The AI assistant never answers it (`neverReason` in `src/ai/common.js`), also when the fill couldn't.

AmWINS, TNI on 2026-10-09 (after a WA run): additional interest is No for every intake, financed or leased vehicles included; a lienholder or lessor is added after the quote. The underwriting questions vehicles not owned, vehicles leased to others, MVR verification, owned unscheduled vehicles and convictions / moving violations are No (`standard`), whatever the intake says about ownership or claims; hazardous material still comes from the intake. A blank years in business (and no years under own authority) is TNI's standard 0 years: "New Venture", established this year; the record stays blank and Check intake warns (`years-in-business.default`), so an answer entered there replaces it. A blank year established is this year also when the years are known (as on the recorded quotes).

Current insurance whose carrier box says "No", "None", "N/A" or starts with "no" ("no POP": no proof of prior) without an expiration date is no prior insurance, whatever type or limit the form shows (`prior.none`, `noPriorInsurance` in `src/util.js`). The policy stays on the record with the carrier as written, so the panel's Current insurance section shows it, but no carrier fills or discloses anything from it: Progressive answers not currently insured, the generic prior carrier and expiration questions are left to the agent with a note (nothing typed), and AmWINS' disclosure has no prior-insurance line. Adding an expiration date in the panel makes it a policy again. A blank carrier box is no answer, not "no prior".

AmWINS "Any prior loss" follows an explicit Yes or No in `claims.hasClaims`. An unknown or missing claims answer leaves prior loss empty with a note and `manualOnly`, so the agent answers it and Luna never receives that unresolved fact.

Carrier rules precede generic rules, and exact matching precedes label fallback. There is no general most-specific-rule resolver; ordering and conditional functions matter. A label fallback is skipped once a carrier rule fills the same intake path, so a carrier rule with a computed value (no path) leaves its path to the generic rule on rows it doesn't answer; a carrier's `rowSections` (AmWINS: drivers, vehicles, vehicle coverages) keeps generic rules out of those sections, and a row the intake doesn't describe stays the agent's. Read [browser filling](browser-filling.md) only when changing matching or execution behavior.

## Adding or correcting a rule

1. Identify the source, target question, carrier/product/state, and affected entity. A similarly worded question is not enough evidence.
2. Classify the answer as an explicit fact, supported transformation, approved scoped default, known non-applicability, or unresolved decision. Unknown is not No.
3. Prefer a shared fact and carrier-specific translation. Add a narrow condition for a real exception; do not copy an entire flow for each combination.
4. Preserve conflicting or ambiguous notes for review. A tentative coverage mention needs confirmation and an entity target before becoming a selection.
5. Test the intended case and a nearby case where the rule must not apply. Update [the AI rulebook](../src/ai/tni-rules.js) when a standard represented there changes.

## Known gaps to account for

Progressive answers question `28C1` (Exclude driver) No for a driver listed on the intake (`derived`); excluding a driver is the agent's decision. AmWINS also has coverage-exclusion defaults. The AI exclusion filter does not protect these deterministic rules. The schema has no dedicated driver-exclusion field; do not describe that case as supported. Non-trucking liability (`coverage.ntl`, set by the agent) is only opened on Progressive ("Add Non-Trucking Liability instead?" on Rates, verified by the link disappearing); what that section asks has not been seen on a live quote, so its questions and the rest of Rates stay with the agent (Rates is never continued for them), and AmWINS has no known NTL option. An NTL mention the agent hasn't answered yet holds the Rates helpers like NTL itself; an explicit No doesn't.

Progressive asks for a Vehicle Type on Add Vehicle only when the VIN lookup doesn't match the tile picked from the intake's type (an intake "Van" whose VIN is a truck tractor); it is left to the agent with a note naming the intake's type. "Zip code where the vehicle is located" (Add Vehicle `2B92`, Add Trailer `2A61`) is the intake's garaging ZIP. With none on the intake, whatever ZIP Progressive put there is left as it is, not asked on every vehicle (TNI, 2026-10-20; `ignore` with an `emptyNote`). A field still empty when the fill ends is the agent's, a reworded question is a check, and an intake ZIP still fills or is flagged when the page says another. Progressive trailers (TNI, 2026-10-07): after the vehicles, Add Trailer, the trailer's tile (Flatbed, Dry Van and Reefer have one; other types are the agent's pick), then the intake trailer's VIN, year, make and value, the vehicle's distance, loan/lease from the intake's trailer ownership (none given, or an answer other than owned, financed or leased, such as Rented: the agent's), comprehensive/collision Yes and no equipment (the intake's trailer equipment value when it gives one); on Rates, its row gets $1,000 comprehensive and collision and no rental or fire/theft. Personal injury protection shares one data-model name across states: Florida gets TNI's standard, other states keep Progressive's choice (an empty one is the agent's). Arkansas asks it in its own control (`PIPInput.Limit`, captioned only "Limit": 5k medical and hospital, income loss and 5k accidental death, alone or together): TNI's standard is 5k Medical and Hospital alone (TNI, 2026-10-19, after a live run where Progressive had picked all three and the control was unfamiliar); other states with that control keep Progressive's choice. On the Print page Email and Fax are unticked, so the agent's Print/Send only opens the PDF. AmWINS has no trailer section (TNI, 2026-10-08): each intake trailer is a line of the Underwriting step's disclosure text, after the radius line, as `Trailer - 2015 Great Dane Dry Van - VIN … - $25K` (year, make and type, VIN, value; a part the intake lacks, or a year or value it couldn't read, is left out). A trailer is never added as a vehicle row or put in the VIN lookup list.

Coverage and ownership are largely shared at policy/operations level. A correctly targeted vehicle row can still receive an inappropriate shared answer. Explicit entity-specific coverage, provenance, applicability/conflict resolution, and final requested-versus-entered reconciliation are design work, not existing guarantees.

For a new missing concept, extend extraction, schema, review UI, rules, and verification together within the authorized scope. If information is absent from the intake, keep a visible agent question rather than inventing an answer, unless TNI has set a standard for it (a `standard` rule, recorded here as TNI's). Repeated missing questions may justify a conditional intake supplement; logs alone do not authorize a new default.
