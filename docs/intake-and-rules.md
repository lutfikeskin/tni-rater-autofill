# Intake mappings and conditional rules

Read this when changing the client record, interpreting an intake, or adding an answer/default/exception.

## Current contract

[intake.js](../src/intake.js) reads named PDF form fields. [normalize.js](../src/normalize.js) builds the record and warnings; [schema.js](../src/schema.js) defines editable fields. Claims-box notes are retained, with limited special parsing such as missing ownership and non-trucking liability. A blank ownership box takes the notes' answer only when it is unambiguous (`ownershipFromNotes`): exactly one ownership word (owned, lease/leased, finance/financed, loan count as mentions; only owned, leased and financed are answers), not negated in its sentence ("not leased; owned outright" names two), with no trailer mention anywhere in the notes ("Trailer.\nFinanced." may describe the trailer; "Trailer leased. Truck owned." names two units); any other mention leaves `operations.vehicleOwnership` empty with an `ownership.notes` check quoting the lines, in place of `ownership.missing`. A filled box always wins over the notes. A line with NTL, non-trucking (also "non tracking") or bobtail adds a review item quoting it, and `coverage.ntl` stays unset until the agent sets it (a mention such as "no bobtail" is not a request). An intake listing a semi trailer (Dry Van, Reefer, Flatbed, Tanker, Lowboy, Step Deck) with no Semi Truck or Pickup Truck to pull it gets a check warning on the vehicle types. This is not a general free-text extractor; Luna's claims-box review (see [AI assistance](ai-assistant.md)) only flags lines.

The intake form changed on 2026-10-19 ([test/fixtures/sample-intake-2026-10.pdf](../test/fixtures/sample-intake-2026-10.pdf); the blank form and a preview are in [docs/intake-form](intake-form/)). The other-insurance-products box (`cross_*`) was removed, the claims box narrowed, and an Additional Notes box (`additional_notes`) added beside it; "Do you take loads to Canada?" (`loads_to_canada`, Yes/No) was added to the FEIN / SR22 / ELD row; and the questions under the trailer rows use the agency's wording, with a single interchange limit and the two equipment values side by side (`trl_interchange_limit`; the older second one beside the hitch, `interchange_limit`, is gone). It keeps the 2026-10-02 form's team driving / slip seating question (`team_driving_or_slip_seating`), which replaced the license plate. Every version is read: `KNOWN_FIELDS` lists the fields of each. `operations.teamDriving` and `operations.canadaLoads` are null (unanswered, not No) on an intake from before the question or when the dropdown is left on Select*. GEICO's team driving or slip seating question (`TeamDrivingOrSlipSeatingYesNo`, Business Class & USDOT) takes `operations.teamDriving`, and a null there is the agent's, with a note; no carrier question is mapped to `operations.canadaLoads` yet. The Additional Notes box is `notes.text`, as written, and is read like the claims box's other lines: `otherNotes` (the claims box's notes and Additional Notes) answers a blank ownership box and feeds the defensive driver and POP-unavailable checks, and `intakeNotes` (the whole claims box and Additional Notes) feeds the NTL mention and Luna's review. Only the claims box's first line answers the claims question. `crossSell` stays on the record for older intakes and is empty on the new form.

The radius box (`parseRadius`) is read like this: "48 states" and "lower 48" alone are long haul (their 48 is not miles); any additional distance is parsed normally; unlimited, long haul, nationwide, OTR and "any" without digits are long haul; a single explicit mileage wins ("anywhere within 100 miles" is 100, "OTR up to 500 miles" is 500). Long haul gives no mileage and no warning, and the carriers pick their widest band (`isLongHaul`, the parser-based predicate they share, used only when there is no mileage; GEICO's is "More than 500"); a bare "48" is 48 miles. Geographic modifiers are bounded to continental/contiguous/US; "48 in-state miles" and "48 interstate miles" remain 48 miles. Location prose after an explicit mile unit ("100 miles in CA") does not turn "in" into inches; standalone inch units remain unresolved. A plain number, with or without "miles", is `operations.radiusMiles`; `operations.radius` keeps the text as written. A range ("100-500 miles", "100 to 500", "100 miles to 500 miles", "100mi-500mi") leaves the miles empty with a `radius.range` check, unsupported or unrecognized distance unit words (kilometres, feet, nautical miles, furlongs, etc.) with `radius.unit`, and anything else ("around town", "11 states", "0", a mileage with another number after it such as "100 miles / 500 miles") with `radius.not-miles`; the check card edits the miles. Long-haul words alongside a range, unsupported unit, zero, or multiple numbers preserve that review item and never select the widest carrier band. GEICO's farthest one-way distance (`GiveFarthestOneWayDistance`, on the vehicle and trailer forms) is the miles as its band (0-25, 26-50, 51-100, 101-200, 201-300, 301-500, More than 500); a band the page doesn't list finds no option and stays the agent's, never the nearest one listed, and a radius with no miles that isn't long haul is the agent's, with a note. An open radius ("500+", "over 300") is only a lower bound, since the reader keeps only its number: from 500 up it is More than 500, and below that the band is the agent's, with a note (miles the agent enters on Check intake count as written).

The owner (`owner()` in [progressive.js](../src/carriers/progressive.js), shared by AmWINS and GEICO) is matched to a driver row for the date of birth and name split by the same full name, or by surname (`ownerIsDriver` in [util.js](../src/util.js)) only when the owner's first name is one of that driver's given names: owner "Alice Sample" is not driver "Bob Sample". When the intake says the owner doesn't drive (`applicant.ownerDrives` false), no row is linked at all, the name parts come from the owner box and the date of birth is the agent's. The owner's date of birth (`owner().dob`: Progressive's Business Owner Info and NoHit, AmWINS' owner DOB, GEICO's owner date of birth) is the matched driver's, or the first of several drivers matching the owner's name. With no driver matching it is never another driver's: it is left to the agent (not the AI) with a warning naming the owner (`ownerDobNote`; TNI, 2026-10-20). Two intake quirks stay: the sole driver whose first name is repeated in the surname box (only when the owner drives) and an owner box holding only a first name, matched to the one driver with it (not when the owner doesn't drive). A company-name box saying there is no company (NO, N/A, NA, N.A., None, Not applicable, No company, with or without a final period) is no company (`companyName` in [util.js](../src/util.js); `applicant.name` null, info note `company.none`; TNI, 2026-10-20). The carriers apply the same rule at quote time, so an agent's edit to "NO" counts too: Progressive's business structure is Individual / Sole Proprietor (its business name box then doesn't show), AmWINS' entity type SOLE PROPRIETOR, and the business name the owner's. With a company name, the carriers read the structure from its last word (`companyStructure`): LLC, or Inc / Incorporated / Corp / Corporation / Company / Co for a corporation (Progressive: Corporation or LLC / Non-Profit; GEICO: Limited Liability Company or Corporation/Other, and Individual/Sole Proprietorship for no company). LLC only inside the name ("SAMPLE LLC DBA EXAMPLE") is filled as LLC with a warning to confirm; any other name ("Express Freight", "SAMPLE CORP TRUCKING") is left to the agent with a warning. A generational suffix written at the end of a driver's first- or last-name box ("SAMPLE, JR", "EXAMPLO III": Jr, Sr, II, III, IV) is read into `drivers[].suffix`, and a lone letter after the first name ("JORDAN L") into `drivers[].middle`, each with an info note (`name.suffix`, `name.middle-initial`) in place of the two-words check (TNI, 2026-10-19, after an Arkansas run where Progressive refused "<first>, JR": its name boxes take letters, digits, spaces, hyphens and apostrophes only). The name boxes then get the name alone. Progressive's and AmWINS' driver pages have no suffix box, so the suffix is left out there; GEICO's Add Driver form has a Suffix list (`GiveDriverSuffix`, optional), which gets `drivers[].suffix` (not yet checked live); Progressive's middle-initial boxes stay as the page has them (its NoHit advice is first and last name only), AmWINS' middle name box gets the initial, and GEICO's forms have no middle name box: its owner first name leaves out a lone middle initial. The owner match counts the suffix (owner "SAMPLE EXAMPLO JR" is driver SAMPLE EXAMPLO, suffix JR). A one-letter first name ("A J") and other two-word first names stay as written, with the check.

A driver's license-number box ending in the word Excluded marks that driver excluded (`drivers[].excluded` true; TNI, 2026-10-20, after a Pennsylvania run where the box's "Excluded" was cleaned into a license number, typed and refused after Continue, while Progressive's exclude question was answered No). The forms read are Exc, Excl, Exclude and Excluded, in any case, with or without a period or in parentheses, as the whole box or after the number ("EXCLUDED", "12345678 EXCLUDED", "D1234567 excl."); "EXCEPT", "EXCLUSION" and the word before the number are read as before. The word is taken off: the license number is what comes before it when that has a digit, cleaned as usual, and otherwise there is none (`license.missing`; "DRIVER EXCLUDED" has none). A `license.excluded` check quotes the box and says what the carriers get (Known gaps, below). The intake has no exclusion question, so `drivers[].excluded` is otherwise null (unanswered, not No); the agent can set it in All client details.

Each answer of the record knows the form field(s) it was read from ([intake-source.js](../src/intake-source.js) `sourceFields`; a driver, vehicle or trailer by its form row, `rows` from `normalizeIntake`, since blank rows are skipped), and the PDF says where each field is drawn (`readIntakePlaces`). The panel uses them to show a check item or a filled intake answer outlined on the intake PDF; a TNI standard or a carrier-only answer has no field and no link. A `derived` answer that fills something carries the intake answers its rule read (`from` on its plan step: `readsOf` in [plan.js](../src/plan.js) runs the rule's value function once more on a view of the record that notes each value read). Blank answers it looked at are left out unless they are all it read, and a list it searched (every driver, for the owner's row) keeps only the items whose values appear in the answer. `sourcePlace` takes those paths and returns one mark per field on the first one's page, plus the box around them. It only locates; it decides no answer, and a rule that throws while traced changes nothing.

A panel edit to `operations.radius` or `applicant.yearEstablished` recomputes what was derived from it (`rederive`: `radiusMiles` through the radius parser, `yearsInBusiness` from the current year) and refreshes that row; an edit to the derived field itself is the agent's value and recomputes nothing, and for the rest of that intake a later edit to its source no longer recomputes it (the panel saves the fields the agent edited with the intake, as `agentEdited`, so they survive closing and reopening the panel, and passes them to `rederive` as `keep`; a newly loaded intake starts with none). Warnings are not recomputed after an edit.

[plan.js](../src/plan.js) resolves values before they enter the page. Rules come from [carriers.js](../src/carriers.js), [carrier modules](../src/carriers/), and [generic rules](../src/rules.js) unless the carrier disables them. Progressive and GEICO disable them (`useGenericRules: false`).

| Rule responsibility | Current mechanism |
| --- | --- |
| Find a question | `field` (data-model name; GEICO's `Id_<Stem>_<n>` by its stem, since GEICO renumbers `<n>` on every redraw) or `ask`/`label` on the rule's `page`, `selector`, `withOption`, or `label` plus `not`; `pageOnly` keeps a data-model name to the rule's pages and `notPages` keeps a rule off pages (a trailer's questions carry the vehicle's names). On a one-address application, `steps` keeps a rule to the steps it answers on (GEICO's progress bar: `GiveFirstName` is the owner on Business & Owner Info and a driver on Drivers & Incidents), and `whenShown` / `unlessShown` (`{ field, label?, unit? }`) to the form of a step that shows, or doesn't show, a question only that form asks |
| Supply an answer | `path` or `value(record, active)`, with its `source` (below) |
| Leave a decision to the agent | `note`; unresolved value becomes a review item |
| Deliberately preserve/skip a field | `ignore`; use only for a known reason |
| Fill but require confirmation | `warn` |
| Address repeated entities | Active list index, `repeat`, selectors, and `scope: 'vehicle'`. With several vehicles, a `scope: 'vehicle'` rule becomes one step per vehicle; each takes the one row naming its vehicle (`vehicleHint`: at least two of year, make and model), and a row's questions without the vehicle in their label belong to the vehicle its labelled questions name. On a page with no vehicle-labelled rows, only the active vehicle's step answers. A vehicle with a trailer on the quote also finds its row by name, and a `scope: 'trailer'` rule (a trailer's Rates row, "2019 Wilson") is one step per trailer, the same way. A `repeat` rule whose rows show no name but a value of the unit names it as a `witness: { selector, path }` (AmWINS' vehicle rows by `[data-id="vin"]` and `vehicles[].vin`): the plan resolves the path for each unit and the step takes the row showing that value, not the row at the unit's position. AmWINS searches all rows for the vehicle value even when the intake has only one vehicle; a unit with no such row (or two) stays the agent's, named without the value. GEICO shows one vehicle's, trailer's or driver's form at a time on one step: a rule whose `whenShown` names a `unit` ('vehicles', 'trailers', 'drivers') answers that form for the panel's active unit, and a form filled for another unit (or a new form while the active unit was filled on another) is held as a check until the agent picks its unit in the panel (`unitForm` / `unitHeld` in the fill report; see [browser filling](browser-filling.md)). `scopeIndex` is copied onto steps but `assign()` does not read it. |
| Interpret existing carrier values | `replace`, `trustPage`, `verifyOnly` (may be a function of the record and active unit: GEICO's vehicle year, make and model are checked against its VIN decode, never typed, only when the vehicle has a VIN), `emptyNote`; `keepChoice`: a question the carrier never pre-selects, so a differing choice already there is kept and flagged, not replaced (`replaceChoices`); `checkNumber`: an `address-suggest` box the carrier filled itself agrees only when it shows the intake's house number, else it is kept and flagged (GEICO's street, from its USDOT record) |

### Answer provenance (`source`)

Every carrier rule with a `value` declares where its answer comes from ([test/provenance.test.mjs](../test/provenance.test.mjs) enforces it); a rule with only a `path` is `intake`. `plan.js fieldSteps` acts on it:

| `source` | Meaning | Step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `intake` | A record path, no computed value | Filled from the intake |
| `derived` | Computed from intake facts, with the evidence in the rule | Filled; may carry its own `warn` or `note` (a missing fact gives a note, never a guess); its step's `from` names the intake answers read, shown on the PDF |
| `standard` | A TNI coverage or workflow standard | Filled and marked `standard: true`; the carrier page marks it blue (an intake answer is green) and the panel's fill report tags it "standard" |
| `fact` | A client fact the intake doesn't supply (loss runs, current-carrier tenure, the year licensed without a readable date of birth, other business coverages when the notes say the client has general liability, …) | Never filled: `value: null`, `manualOnly: true` (the AI assistant leaves it) and the rule's `note` (else "<name> isn't on the intake: answer it yourself") |
| `attestation` | Something the agent must assert (the AmWINS declaration, Progressive continuous coverage when the intake doesn't show the customer currently insured, Progressive "USDOT belongs to the business" when the intake has no business or owner name to check) | Never filled, as `fact` |

A rule's `source` may be a function of the record when the intake decides it. "No equipment" (Progressive's vehicle and trailer "has no equipment", on Add Vehicle, Add Trailer and the trailer's Rates row, and AmWINS "Permanently attached equipment" No) is `standard`, TNI's like the $0 to $2,000 band, when the intake gives no equipment value (blank or unreadable, such as N/A), so it is tagged and becomes a check item in review mode; with a value on the intake it is `derived` (Progressive: up to $2,000 Yes, more No; AmWINS: $0 No, any other amount the agent's).

A rule marked `clientFact` (AmWINS marital status, entity type, prior loss, attached equipment; Progressive business structure, has a USDOT number and vehicle / trailer loan / lease; GEICO current GEICO policy, has a USDOT number, filing type, owner date of birth and marital status, ownership type, customizations, a driver's relationship to the business, current auto insurance and the liability insurance the business has) answers from the intake when it can; when it can't (no value, or a note), its step is `manualOnly` too, so the AI assistant never suggests or picks it.

Review mode: a carrier lists the business classes its rules were written for (`supportedClasses`, the same patterns the class-dependent answers use; Progressive: an expediter or a freight hauler class ("Freight Hauler", "General Freight Hauling"), as its for-hire and business-type answers read it, so "General Freight" or a Dry Van hauling general freight fills in review mode; AmWINS: the class or what they haul names expediting or general freight, the same pattern its freight answers (484122, TRANSPORTATION, FOR HIRE) use, so a "Freight Hauler" of furniture gets both their notes and review mode). `supportedClass(record, carrier)` is false for any other class and for a missing one; then every `standard` step gets the `warn` "Review mode: the business class "…" isn't validated for <carrier>; confirm this standard answer.", so it is filled but becomes a check item that holds Continue, and the panel shows a review-mode banner above the fill button. A retained carrier choice also requires confirmation in review mode, even when it differs from the standard: its value is preserved and Continue waits for the agent to acknowledge it. Intake and derived answers are unaffected. GEICO lists Progressive's (an expediter or a freight hauler class), the quotes its TNI standards (below) were set on; any other class fills them in review mode.

- A driver besides the owner(s) is an Employee.
- The number of employees is the intake's drivers besides the owner(s), as GEICO's band (none, 1, 2-3, …).
- Years with the current auto insurer is 1-3 Years when the intake shows a current auto policy (proof of prior insurance).
- The other liability insurance the business has is None, unless the intake shows a current GL policy (GL, `derived`) or you marked on Check intake that the client has GL (the agent's: GL or a business owner's policy).
- A named additional insured or waiver of subrogation, and a contract needing a blanket additional insured or blanket waiver of subrogation endorsement, are No.
- In Florida, Medical Payments is Not Included (PIP covers it) and the PIP lists (stacked uninsured motorist, basic PIP, PIP options, excluded work loss) are left as GEICO has them; elsewhere they are the agent's.
- Refrigeration breakdown is Yes with a reefer on the intake's vehicles or trailers and No without one, when the intake doesn't answer it; an intake No with a reefer listed is the agent's.
- On the gateway's Search Products window, the product is Commercial Auto/Trucking: its box is ticked, and no other product is touched (TNI, FL live run 3, 2026-10-21).

Every other question is the agent's: a `fact` or `attestation` with a note, or, for a pick from a long list (the business descriptions of any other class, a vehicle type other than a cargo van or a semi truck, the trailer type), a note quoting the intake's words, which the AI assistant may suggest for the agent's approval. Progressive's and AmWINS' other standards don't carry over: on GEICO a blank hazardous material placard, team driving answer, years in business, cargo deductible or GL limit is the agent's, as are whether GEICO's USDOT record is the client's business (`attestation`), cost new, uninsured motorist, medical payments outside Florida, the other vehicle coverage rows (fire and theft, rental, roadside), the payment plan, the hired and non-owned auto questions and the GL follow-ups. Gross vehicle weight is left as GEICO fills it (TNI: GEICO's value stands). The owner's Social Security number is never filled. TNI stops a GEICO quote at Quote & Coverages and prints the quote proposal there: Final Quote Details leads to MVR & CLUE, whose reports cost the agency, so Final Quote Details, MVR & CLUE and Payment Information are the agent's steps (`stopSteps`: nothing is filled or clicked there, and their notes say to go back and print), and the FEIN and license numbers are not mapped, nor are incidents on Drivers & Incidents (`claims.hasClaims`). The quote starts on the agents' gateway (gateway2.geico.com): its Start New Quote window, drawn over any gateway page, takes the garaging ZIP (five digits), the dashboard's USDOT check and the Search Products window's USDOT box the USDOT number when the intake has one, and Search Products' product is Commercial Auto/Trucking (above); nothing else there is filled (each of the gateway's own controls has its own ignore rule, since a selector rule answers its first match only), and no layout of it is recorded. With a VIN, the vehicle form's answers wait for GEICO's decoded year (`waitFor`), since the decode clears what was given before it.

GEICO's derived answers: its ZIP boxes take five digits (a ZIP+4 is cut to its ZIP; anything else is the agent's); the business street: the intake's street, city, state and ZIP typed (the unit has boxes of its own), and GEICO's suggestion with the same house number and ZIP picked; with none, the street alone is left in the box for the agent to pick, and a street GEICO already shows (from its USDOT record) is kept when it has the intake's house number and flagged when not (FL live run 3, 2026-10-21); whether there is a USDOT number (Yes with one; No only when the intake says none is coming within 60 days and has no MC number; "Not yet, but the customer has applied for one" is never derived); a current GEICO commercial auto policy (No for no prior insurance, a personal auto policy or a commercial auto policy with another carrier; Yes only for a current GEICO commercial auto policy); the owner's names, date of birth and marital status through `owner()`, never the first driver; the ownership type from the company name by the rule Progressive and AmWINS share (`companyStructure`, above: its last word LLC, or Inc / Corp / Company; LLC inside the name with a warning; no company, or a box saying there is none, a sole proprietor when the intake names the owner; Partnership and Trust are never guessed); the unit number and type; customizations from the attached equipment value ($0 is No for every vehicle; a value is Yes, and the amount, only with one vehicle, or one trailer; none is the standard No, above); the distance band (above); personal use (No for all; Yes only with one vehicle) and SR-22 (No for all; Yes only when GEICO lists one driver: on the owner's form, the owner matched to the intake's only driver; on Add Driver, the intake's only driver when the owner doesn't drive); a driver's relationship to the business (Owner for one of the intake's owners; any other driver is the standard Employee, above); Vehicle or Trailer on the chooser behind Add Vehicle or Trailer, for the intake's next vehicle, then trailer, not on the quote (the side panel reads it from the vehicle list; with every intake unit there it is the agent's); the years-in-business bucket; current auto insurance and its bodily injury limits (exact matches only, never the closest); the GL policy the business has; the liability coverage (Bodily Injury and Property Damage Liability for an auto liability request; the agent's with NTL); cargo as GEICO's limit and deductible pair; household goods Yes for a Household Goods Mover; and the filing kind and boxes from the filings the intake names (SR-22 and FR-44 alone don't count; federal liability is BMC-91, MCS-90 or a federal liability filing, and "federal" next to a cargo filing such as BMC-34 is the agent's; a box no filing names is never unticked).

Carrier rules precede generic rules, and exact matching precedes label fallback. There is no general most-specific-rule resolver; ordering and conditional functions matter. A label fallback is skipped once a carrier rule fills the same intake path, so a carrier rule with a computed value (no path) leaves its path to the generic rule on rows it doesn't answer; a carrier's `rowSections` (AmWINS: drivers, vehicles, vehicle coverages) keeps generic rules out of those sections, and a row the intake doesn't describe stays the agent's. Progressive and GEICO get no generic rules at all. Read [browser filling](browser-filling.md) only when changing matching or execution behavior.

## Adding or correcting a rule

1. Identify the source, target question, carrier/product/state, and affected entity. A similarly worded question is not enough evidence.
2. Classify the answer as an explicit fact, supported transformation, approved scoped default, known non-applicability, or unresolved decision. Unknown is not No.
3. Prefer a shared fact and carrier-specific translation. Add a narrow condition for a real exception; do not copy an entire flow for each combination.
4. Preserve conflicting or ambiguous notes for review. A tentative coverage mention needs confirmation and an entity target before becoming a selection.
5. Test the intended case and a nearby case where the rule must not apply. Update [the AI rulebook](../src/ai/tni-rules.js) when a standard represented there changes.

## Known gaps to account for

Progressive answers question `28C1` (Exclude driver) No for a driver listed on the intake and Yes for one the intake marks excluded (`drivers[].excluded`, above; `derived`, TNI, 2026-10-20). What Progressive asks after a Yes has not been seen on a live quote. AmWINS has no driver-exclusion question (its "Exclude …" boxes are coverage exclusions, with defaults): an excluded driver's license number gets "(Excluded)" after it ("12345678 (Excluded)", TNI, 2026-10-20). With no license number on the intake that box is the agent's, with a note to type the number and "(Excluded)". GEICO's Add Driver form asks nothing about exclusion: an excluded driver is filled, with a warning to handle the exclusion by hand. The AI exclusion filter does not protect these deterministic rules. An exclusion written anywhere but at the end of the license-number box (the notes, "EXCLUDED DRIVER", the word before the number) is not read; do not describe that case as supported. Non-trucking liability (`coverage.ntl`, set by the agent) is only opened on Progressive ("Add Non-Trucking Liability instead?" on Rates, verified by the link disappearing); what that section asks has not been seen on a live quote, so its questions and the rest of Rates stay with the agent (Rates is never continued for them), and AmWINS has no known NTL option. An NTL mention the agent hasn't answered yet holds the Rates helpers like NTL itself; an explicit No doesn't. On GEICO, NTL set to Yes, or mentioned and not yet answered, leaves Liability Coverage and its limit to the agent.

Progressive asks for a Vehicle Type on Add Vehicle only when the VIN lookup doesn't match the tile picked from the intake's type (an intake "Van" whose VIN is a truck tractor); it is left to the agent with a note naming the intake's type. "Zip code where the vehicle is located" (Add Vehicle `2B92`, Add Trailer `2A61`) is the intake's garaging ZIP. With none on the intake, whatever ZIP Progressive put there is left as it is, not asked on every vehicle (TNI, 2026-10-20; `ignore` with an `emptyNote`). A field still empty when the fill ends is the agent's, a reworded question is a check, and an intake ZIP still fills or is flagged when the page says another. Progressive trailers (TNI, 2026-10-07): after the vehicles, Add Trailer, the trailer's tile (Flatbed, Dry Van and Reefer have one; other types are the agent's pick), then the intake trailer's VIN, year, make and value, the vehicle's distance, loan/lease from the intake's trailer ownership (none given, or an answer other than owned, financed or leased, such as Rented: the agent's), comprehensive/collision Yes and no equipment (the intake's trailer equipment value when it gives one); on Rates, its row gets $1,000 comprehensive and collision and no rental or fire/theft. Personal injury protection shares one data-model name across states: Florida gets TNI's standard, other states keep Progressive's choice (an empty one is the agent's). Arkansas asks it in its own control (`PIPInput.Limit`, captioned only "Limit": 5k medical and hospital, income loss and 5k accidental death, alone or together): TNI's standard is 5k Medical and Hospital alone (TNI, 2026-10-19, after a live run where Progressive had picked all three and the control was unfamiliar); other states with that control keep Progressive's choice. On the Print page Email and Fax are unticked, so the agent's Print/Send only opens the PDF. AmWINS has no trailer section (TNI, 2026-10-08): each intake trailer is a line of the Underwriting step's disclosure text, after the radius line, as `Trailer - 2015 Great Dane Dry Van - VIN … - $25K` (year, make and type, VIN, value; a part the intake lacks, or a year or value it couldn't read, is left out). A trailer is never added as a vehicle row or put in the VIN lookup list. GEICO (not yet checked live): the agent clicks Next on every step and opens Add Vehicle or Trailer and Add Driver; each vehicle's or trailer's form gets that unit's answers (the panel's active one), and where it is parked gets the intake's garaging ZIP and city while its street stays as GEICO has it (the agent's only when empty). No rule is written for a trailer's rows on Quote & Coverages, so they stay the agent's.

Coverage and ownership are largely shared at policy/operations level. A correctly targeted vehicle row can still receive an inappropriate shared answer. Explicit entity-specific coverage, provenance, applicability/conflict resolution, and final requested-versus-entered reconciliation are design work, not existing guarantees.

For a new missing concept, extend extraction, schema, review UI, rules, and verification together within the authorized scope. If information is absent from the intake, keep a visible agent question rather than inventing an answer, unless TNI has set a standard for it (a `standard` rule, recorded here as TNI's). Repeated missing questions may justify a conditional intake supplement; logs alone do not authorize a new default.
