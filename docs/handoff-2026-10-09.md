## Retained boundaries and limitations

- Missing client facts and attestations stay with the agent; explicit No and
  not applicable remain distinct from missing information. Unsupported classes
  require review of standard answers. See [intake and rules](intake-and-rules.md)
  and [AI assistance](ai-assistant.md).
- Preserve manual corrections, retained-answer checks, and Continue blockers.
  Do not bind, purchase, submit, order reports, or sign. See
  [browser filling](browser-filling.md).
- The protection ledger does not distinguish an intentionally blank answer
  from one lost during a redraw; the protected-blank/Ignore case remains open.
  A stale VIN decode matching the intake's year, make, and model can also pass
  the existing checks.
- The 100-case intake pool is ignored local data, not part of a clone. Its
  historical generator retains real VIN exceptions, including a source-intake
  identifier, so the pool is not a wholly synthetic fixture suitable for adding
  to Git unchanged. The intake runner needs its manifest, selected PDFs, and
  matching lookup JSON. Missing pool data is a limitation, not a passing test.
  See [testing](testing.md) for `--pool` and case selection.
- Keep raw carrier pages, PDFs, client captures, credentials, local server data,
  and generated reports outside Git. A clone does not transfer other
  worktrees' uncommitted changes or local extension settings.
