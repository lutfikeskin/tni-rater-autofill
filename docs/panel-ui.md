# Panel UI

Read this when a change adds or alters what the side panel draws: a view (`sidepanel/panel/*-view.jsx`), its markup in `sidepanel/sidepanel.html`, or `sidepanel/sidepanel.css`. It holds the rules every view follows so that an agent using a keyboard or a screen reader gets what a mouse user gets. What the panel shows and when is in [Browser filling](browser-filling.md#review-and-navigation); how its views are tested is in [Testing](testing.md).

Several of these rules come from comparing the panel with [vercel-labs/ui](https://github.com/vercel-labs/ui) (UI SDK, a library of keyboard, focus and ARIA behaviors, 2026-11). The panel doesn't ship that library; it keeps its own views and styles and follows the same rules.

## Structure

The panel's document never scrolls (`overflow: clip` on `html` and `body`); only `<main id="view">` in the middle and `<footer class="foot">` in a short window may scroll. Above `<main id="view">`, `header.panel-head` holds `.top`, `section#quote` and `.top-right` on one CSS grid so the top bar and context line stay in the same place across every state:

- **Top bar**: the client's name disclosure (`#client-card`, opening `#details-view`) when `#quote` is shown, or `#brand` ("Rater Autofill") when no intake is shown or Settings is open, paired on the right with `#pop-out`, `#dock-back` and `#settings-btn`.
- **Context line**: `#carrier-line` (`#site-logo` or `#site`, `#site-step`, `#site-count` "Stage n of m", `#site-caption`, written by `renderSite` and separated with middots in CSS) when a rater tab is in front, or `#client-meta` (the intake's state and counts, from `client-view.jsx`) when an intake is loaded without a rater tab in front.
- **2px progress rule**: `#site-rule` (`rule-view.jsx`), drawn from `state.siteTrack` (completed steps over total steps). It is `aria-hidden="true"` because the context line above carries the stage in words; `#site-track` (`site-view.jsx`) remains in the DOM, visually hidden, for `renderSite`.
- **Client tools**: on the top bar beside the window controls, `.client-tools` holds the client's tools in one compact toolbar (`.btn-group`, `role="toolbar"` "Client tools" with a single roving Tab stop and `ArrowLeft`/`ArrowRight`/`Home`/`End`, outlined in grey with dividers between its buttons and subtle tactile shading; Intake carries the warm amber Intake tint): **Intake** (`#open-pdf`, only with the PDF kept), **Summary** (`#quote-summary`), **Change file** (`#replace`), **New quote** (`#new-quote`) and **Clear** (`#clear-client`), each an icon and its word; below 680px only the icons show, each button still named by its `aria-label`. Clear's word and icon are red. Before Change file, New quote or Clear lets the agent's work go, `#file-confirm` asks in a `role="alertdialog" aria-modal="true"` popover under the button that asked (a title naming the question, its text, Cancel and the answer, red for Clear), moving focus to Cancel on open and wrapping Tab between its buttons, its arrow pointing at that button; Escape or a click outside it is Cancel and returns focus to the trigger. The context line (`#client-meta`) shows the state, then the drivers, vehicles and trailers each as an icon and its number, its words for a screen reader. A checked intake item's Undo is an undo-arrow icon button named "Undo: <the check>".
- **View tabs & intake checks**: `#steps` (`steps-view.jsx`, `role="tablist"` "Intake or this page") draws both views as equal-height APG tabs (`role="tab"`, `aria-selected`, roving Tab stop with `ArrowLeft`/`ArrowRight`/`Home`/`End`, paired with `role="tabpanel"` on `#check-view` and `#page-view`) on a sunken strip, always shown: `#step-check` ("Intake checks", its note `#check-summary` "N to check" in amber while checks are open, else "All checked") and `#step-fill` ("This page", its note `#fill-summary`), the selected tab styled with a color-coded full outline and tint (warm amber `--amber-soft` `#fef6dc` with `--warning-bar` border on `#step-check`, brand blue `--brand-soft` `#eaf3fc` with `--brand` border on `#step-fill`) that blends seamlessly into the matching `.view` canvas with no horizontal divider seam under the active tab (the `1.5px` colored border runs on `.steps` under the inactive tab while the active tab's `::after` bridges into `.view`), while the inactive tab keeps a softer matching tint and grey/amber border so neither disappears into the strip. Intake-linked buttons (`See intake`, `See in intake`, and `#see-all-checks`) share the Intake tab's warm amber tinted style (`.intake-action`). On `#check-view` (`checks-view.jsx`), open intake checks draw one at a time in focus mode (`FocusCheck`, `#warnings.checks-focus`, as an elevated white card with an amber header band and circular corner `?`, equal-weight `ghost` choice buttons, `#check-later` in the card's footer focusing the dock's enabled primary button on the page view without starting a fill, and crimson `Serious` eyebrow wearing `role="alert"` + `.check-serious-note` announced once via `useAnnounce` + `#check-later` "Decide later and fill" on an `alert` check while filling stays enabled); `#see-all-checks` sits as a full-width warm-amber button inside the bottom `.tools-card` (above `#details-row`) whenever `total > 1 || cleanup.length > 0`, and `#see-all-checks` and `#one-check-mode` toggle `state.checksAll` via `act` (reset when a new intake is loaded or cleared) and keep focus on the opposite toggle, and marking a check moves focus to the Undo button in list mode or the next open check's first control in focus mode.
- **Canvas screen head & heading hierarchy**: `sidepanel.html` keeps `<h1 class="sr-only">TNI Rater Autofill</h1>` as the document's single `<h1>`, and `#screen-head` inside `#screen-root` (`screen-view.jsx`) draws `#screen-eyebrow` (`data-tone` matching `.dock-eyebrow`), `<h2 id="screen-title" tabIndex={-1}>` (`17px`), and `#screen-lead` (`13px`). It shows on the 17 canvas screens (`start`, `norater`, `unreadable`, `preview`, `portal`, `stopped`, `yours`, `hold`, `defaults`, `owner`, `ready`, `filling`, `elsewhere`, `stopped-fill`, `error`, `finished`, `continued`), hiding `#feed-title` while visible so two headings never stack above the live fill list, visually collapsing on `owner` when the owner-check banner card is shown (and skipping `#dock[data-head-out]` so the owner check isn't repeated), and hides on `check`, `review`, `ai`, `settings` and when `#pdf-view`, `#summary-view` or `#details-view` is open. When `screenFor(state)` transitions to a new visible canvas screen while the panel has focus (`document.hasFocus()`) on a non-interactive, non-dock panel element (and is not closing Settings), focus moves to `#screen-title` and scrolls it into view with `scrollBehavior()`. On Quote done (`finished`), `#done-facts` under the lead lists the summary sheet's counts as hairline rows (`summaryNow`, read once each time the dock is drawn there): Answers in, Left for you (amber when not 0), Marked done without an answer (its own line, never counted as answered) and Changed by you.
- **Dock & scrolled-off screen head**: `#dock` (`steps-view.jsx`) keeps `#dock-eyebrow` and `#dock-title` in the DOM alongside `#dock-sub`, `#dock-paused`, `#fill-progress`, the 38px primary button (`#fill`, `#fill-stop`, `#dock-next`, `#look-right`, `#ai-approve`, `#dock-defaults`, `#dock-summary`) and `#dock-alt`, hiding `#dock` only when `!state.dock` or while Settings is open without Stop. While `#screen-head` is shown, `#dock-eyebrow` and `#dock-title` keep `aria-hidden="true"` so the title appears once in the accessibility tree, wearing `sr-only` (with `#dock-sub` hidden) while `#screen-title` is in full view and revealing visually (with `aria-hidden="true"` on `#dock-sub`) when `IntersectionObserver` (`threshold: 1` on `#screen-title`) marks `#dock[data-head-out]`; on `check`, `review` and `ai`, the dock shows its eyebrow, title and sub without `aria-hidden`.
- **Unified post-fill page sheet & Option B answer trays**: on the active tab's seamlessly blended view canvas (`--brand-soft` `#eaf3fc` on `This page`, `--amber-soft` `#fef6dc` on `Intake checks`; neutral slate-mist `--canvas` `#eef2f6` on Home and Settings), post-fill answers render in ONE unified view (`feed-view.jsx` + `report-view.jsx`; `review-view.jsx` is retired and renders `null`) instead of competing summary, focused-need, and all-answers screens. A quiet borderless `.feed-head` holds `<h2 id="feed-title">` (the current page title, e.g. `Household Members`, hidden while `#screen-head` is visible), `#page-menu` (earlier pages filled on the tab), `#key-btn` (answer tags & page-marks key), `?` (`HelpLink`), and `.feed-sub` (`"N filled · M needs you"` or `"N filled · all set"`). Directly below sits one elevated white tabbed sheet (`.feed-sheet`, `#c5d8ec` border) with a 3-tab `#filters` toolbar (`role="toolbar"`): **Needs** (with a 7px amber dot when `needs > 0`), **Worth a look** (`edited`, `changed`, `own`, settled needs, and `standard` rows marked `data-worth="1"` via `worthRank`), and **All** (showing an inline live filter input `#feed-search-input` when a page has > 6 fields, hiding non-matching rows via `data-search-hide="1"` and resetting on tab/report switch). `renderReport` automatically selects `defaultFilter(list)`: `needs` when unsettled needs or pending AI picks exist, `worth` when 0 needs and worth-a-look rows exist, else `all`. Inside `#report`, every `AnswerRow` and `NeedRow` shares the **Option B Accent Bar + Inset Tray** anatomy: crisp 1-line collapsed rows (`Label` left, bold `Value` right + chevron); expanding any row in place reveals a `3px` color-coded left accent bar (`data-tone`: blue default, amber `need`, green `you`, violet `ai`, red `bad`), stacked `Label → Answer` with source `<Tag>` on the right, and a framed inset `.row-tray` (`.row-tray-info` + `.row-tray-foot`). Unsettled needs keep full action buttons (`Show on page` primary, `Mark done` ghost, plus `Correct it in client details` `.vin-fix` on vehicle/trailer VIN holds, and `.mark-done-note` linked via `aria-describedby` explaining `Mark done` without calling it answered) and quiet violet `AiCard` callouts; filled answers and settled needs keep slim `.tray-link` footer actions (`Change answer`, warm-amber `See in intake`, `Show on page`, and inline `Undo` on settled notes). For a report kept while another page was in front, `#report-age` sits inside the sheet above `#report`. The integrated `.feed-tools` footer holds `Clear page marks` (`#unmark`) and the total field count (`.feed-total`), keeping DOM and Tab order aligned top to bottom.
- **Settings' How it runs**: under Settings' head, `#settings-mode` (`settings-view.jsx`) says in one sentence how the Filling switches run the quote (`modeSaid` in `settings-summary.js`, words in `messages.js` `SETTINGS`), derived from `state.switches` on each draw and never stored; every switch keeps its own visible description below.
- **Sheets and Report a problem**: the PDF and summary sheets and the client details drawer keep `role="dialog"` `aria-modal="true"` and their closes, with a 17px title in `.sheet-head`, hairline rows for the summary's lists, and a full-width 38px primary in `.sheet-foot` beside its quiet secondary. `#problem-form` (in the footer, `role="group"` named by its `<h2 id="problem-title">` "Report a problem") leads with one sentence, keeps its kind select, a 13.5px box and a full-width Save report after Cancel.
- **Base line**: `.foot-row` at the bottom of `<footer class="foot">` on the grey strip, orders `#recording-root` ("Recording usage"), `#report-problem` (a white tab hanging from the dock above: light grey edges on its sides and bottom, none where it joins) and `#foot-version` (visually hidden below 360px, since Settings shows `#version`).

## Saying what changed

A screen reader reads a live region (`role="status"`, `aria-live`) when its words change, but often not when the region itself is shown, or put on the page, at the moment its words go in. So the panel has one live region, `#announce` in `sidepanel.html` (`role="status"`, `aria-live="polite"`, visually hidden, never `hidden`), and everything it says goes there:

- Plain code calls `announce(text)` from [announce.js](../sidepanel/panel/announce.js) (`coordinator.js` imports it for the rest of the panel). It empties the region and fills it a moment later (`ANNOUNCE_MS`), so the same words said again are read again; words said in that moment are read together, in order, each once, instead of the last replacing the others.
- A note in a React view that shows and hides (`hidden={!text}`) has no live role of its own. It calls `useAnnounce(text, ref)` with a ref to its element, which says the words each time they change to new words, unless the note is inside a hidden part of the panel (Settings closed, a sheet not open). Today these are the client's note (`#client-note`), `FocusCheck`'s serious note (`.check-serious-note`), the dock's paused line (`#dock-paused`), the quote summary's saved line (`#summary-saved`), Settings' setup note (`#setup-note`), its note while answers go in (`#filling-now-note`), the capture status (`#capture-status`) and the developer recording's status (`#devrec-status`); the footer's line about a problem report (`#problem-said`, `sayProblem` in `usage-panel.js`) calls `announce` itself.
- A line that is always on the page and only changes its words can keep `aria-live` (Settings' usage note, `#usage-note`).
- A card that must interrupt (a fill that stopped, a PDF that can't be read) keeps `role="alert"`.

Don't add `role="status"` to an element that is drawn hidden or mounted with its words; use `useAnnounce` instead. `test/panel/announce.test.jsx` checks the region's timing and the hook. The issue cards drawn by `cards-view.jsx` and `notes-view.jsx` still take `role="status"` from their card's `role` as they are put on the page; moving them to `announce` is a change of its own.

## Over the panel

Five things cover part of the panel: the PDF sheet, the quote summary and client details (sheets, `role="dialog"` with `aria-modal="true"`), the file popover (`#file-confirm`, counted only while it shows: Settings hides it), and Settings, which covers the view but leaves the header in use. The keyboard over them is one function, `overlayKey` in [overlay-keys.js](../sidepanel/panel/overlay-keys.js), which `sidepanel.js` calls on every keydown with the layers in that order:

- Escape closes the topmost layer open, and only that one, through the panel's own close (`closePdf`, `closeSummary`, `closeDetails`, the popover's Cancel, `closeSettings`), which gives focus back to the button that opened it.
- Tab and Shift+Tab stay inside an open sheet, wrapping at its ends; Tab from outside it goes to its first stop. The stops are links, enabled buttons and boxes, `summary` and elements with a `tabindex`, that are shown (`checkVisibility`). Settings isn't modal: Tab leaves it as it would anywhere.
- Opening a sheet moves focus into it (`openDetails`, `openSummary`, `openPdf`). Opening Settings leaves focus on its button, which says `aria-expanded="true"`; the next Tab goes into it.
- Settings closed with focus inside it gives focus back to `#settings-btn`, however it closed: Done, Escape, its button (`closeSettings`), or a new intake loaded while it was open (`showScreen`).

A new layer goes into that list in `sidepanel.js`, at its place in the order, with its own close that gives focus back. `test/panel/overlay-keys.test.jsx` and the safety tests check this.

## Names

Every control a keyboard or screen-reader user can reach has an accessible name: its own text, its `<label>`, or `aria-label` on a button that shows only an icon (the header's Settings, the client strip's tools, a sheet's Close). A name says what the control does where it sits, and two controls side by side don't share one: Change file and New quote with this intake, not two "Intake" buttons. `test/panel/control-names.test.jsx` asks Testing Library for a nameless control of every interactive role in `sidepanel.html` (with the quote and the problem form shown) and in each view; one drawn hidden isn't asked.

## Lists

A list whose items can change while it is on screen is keyed by what each item is, never by its place: a card's buttons by their label, a suggestion's rules by their text, a report's rows by their record, the live list by its field. Keyed by place, an item that goes away hands its node, with the agent's focus or an open fold, to the item after it. A list drawn once and never changed (a report's head, an `<option>` list whose value is its place) may use its place. `test/panel/cards-view.test.jsx` checks that a card said again without one of its buttons keeps focus on the button the agent was on.

## Motion

With the system's reduced-motion setting on, nothing in the panel slides, spins or fades: one rule at the end of `sidepanel.css` turns off every animation and transition, on elements and on their `::before` and `::after` content (the 2px `#site-rule` width transition included), and the fill's bar and spinner change to a steady full bar and a still "…" so they don't read as stuck. Scrolling the panel does it too: every `scrollIntoView` takes its `behavior` from `scrollBehavior()` (`coordinator.js`), `auto` with reduced motion and `smooth` otherwise, never a fixed `smooth`. `test/panel-contrast.test.mjs` checks the CSS, and the safety tests check Settings' scroll to a box.

## Checked against their pattern

A control the panel builds itself (a switch, checkbox, disclosure, tablist, toolbar, or alertdialog) is held to its WAI-ARIA pattern in `test/panel/aria-patterns.test.jsx`, with `checkPattern` from `@ui-sdk/core/testing`, a development dependency pinned exactly that nothing in the extension imports:

- A switch is an `input type="checkbox"` with `role="switch"` and a name (its row's label), and Space turns it on and off; plain checkboxes in Settings follow the APG checkbox pattern.
- A disclosure is a named button with `aria-expanded` `true` or `false` and an `aria-controls` that names a real element (`#client-card`, `#details-row`, `#key-btn`, `#key-edit`, `#defaults-chip`, and `ReportRows` `.row-head` on both `AnswerRow` and `NeedRow`); Enter and Space open it and close it again. A button that only opens something (a second press asks again) isn't a disclosure: Clear log's `aria-expanded` is the one left out of the check for that.
- `#steps` is a named `role="tablist"` with `role="tab"` buttons (`aria-selected`, `aria-controls`, roving Tab stop, `ArrowLeft`/`ArrowRight`/`Home`/`End`, `Enter`/`Space`) paired with `role="tabpanel"` (`aria-labelledby`) on `#check-view` and `#page-view`.
- Client tools (`.btn-group`) and feed filters (`#filters`) are named `role="toolbar"` composites with one roving Tab stop and `ArrowLeft`/`ArrowRight`/`Home`/`End`.
- `#file-confirm` is a `role="alertdialog" aria-modal="true"` popover (`aria-labelledby`, `aria-describedby`) that focuses Cancel on open, traps Tab/Shift+Tab between its buttons, and closes on Escape back to its trigger.

A new control of these kinds gets its `checkPattern` there, with its button's act given as the panel does it. A sheet's Tab and Escape are the panel's ([Over the panel](#over-the-panel)), so a view alone can't pass the dialog pattern.

## Tooltips

A `title` shows only to a mouse user who rests on the control: keyboard and touch users never see it (audit 2026-11-07, UX-10). So what an agent needs in order to decide goes in visible text: a switch's or checkbox's meaning under its name (Settings' Filling switches, the test-run mark `#test-run-about`, the developer's switch for your defaults `#defaults-dev-about`), a fold's purpose as its first line (`#defaults-about`, `#defaults-advanced-about`), a key as a line under its button (`#capture-key`, with `aria-keyshortcuts`), and `NeedRow`'s `.mark-done-note` explaining what `Mark done` leaves untouched without calling the question answered. The control points at that line with `aria-describedby`, so a screen reader says it with the control.

A `title` may stay where it repeats the control's name for a mouse user (an icon button's `aria-label`, as on the header's buttons and a sheet's Close) or adds a hint to a control whose name and visible text already say what it does (the client strip's tools, a row's Show on page and Mark done, the answer tags, which the answer key explains). A control's name never rests on its `title` alone: an icon button has its `aria-label` in every state (the PDF sheet's Fit page before the zoom is known included); `test/panel/control-names.test.jsx` asks for names with every `title` taken away.
