Usage log

While an intake is loaded, this extension records how it's used on Progressive and GEICO pages, to improve it: which intake (by fingerprint, not name), which fields it filled, what you changed afterwards, and problems you report.

Not recorded: client names, numbers or anything you type into text boxes (typing is only compared with the intake on this computer), screenshots, keystrokes, or other websites.
